Delete Your Aurora Chess Account

You can permanently delete your Aurora Chess account and associated data at any time. This page explains what gets deleted, what is kept, and how to request deletion.

Option 1 — In-app deletion (fastest)

  1. Open Aurora Chess on any platform.
  2. Tap your avatar badge to open your profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap DELETE ACCOUNT…
  4. Type your username to confirm, then tap DELETE ACCOUNT.
  5. Your account is removed immediately from this device. Server-side data is purged within 30 days.

Option 2 — Email request

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your account:

To: privacy@aurora-chess.com
Subject: Account Deletion Request
Body: Your in-game username and platform (Web / Android / Steam / Xbox / iOS).

We process email requests within 30 days (GDPR Art. 12 timeline).

What gets deleted

  • Your username, hashed password, and email
  • ELO rating and match history
  • Friends list and friend requests
  • Linked Google / Steam / Xbox account IDs
  • In-app settings and puzzle progress stored server-side

What is kept

  • Anonymized match records in other players' histories (your username is replaced with "deleted_user")
  • Aggregated statistics that cannot identify you
  • Backups for up to 30 days, after which they are also purged

Platform-specific notes

Google Play: Deleting your app-side account does not delete your Google account. Revoke OAuth access separately at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Steam: Steam achievements and playtime remain on your Steam profile; only in-game account data is deleted.
